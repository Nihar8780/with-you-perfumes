Shipping Policy

Fast, secure, and reliable delivery for every luxury fragrance.

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days after payment confirmation. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed on the next business day.

Standard delivery usually takes 3–7 business days, depending on your location. Delivery times may vary during peak seasons.

3. Shipping Charges

We offer FREE shipping on eligible orders. Any applicable shipping charges will be displayed during checkout.

4. Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, you'll receive an email and SMS with your tracking number and courier details.

5. Delivery Issues

If your package is delayed, lost, or arrives damaged, please contact our customer support within 48 hours.
