Product Description

Creed Aventus is one of the world's most iconic luxury fragrances. It opens with refreshing notes of pineapple, bergamot and blackcurrant before evolving into a sophisticated heart of birch, jasmine and patchouli. The fragrance finishes with rich musk, oakmoss and vanilla for exceptional longevity and elegance.

Designed for individuals who appreciate confidence, sophistication and timeless luxury, this fragrance is perfect for business meetings, evening events, weddings and special occasions.
