Return & Refund Policy

Our commitment to a secure and transparent shopping experience.

1. Return Eligibility

Products may be returned within 7 days of delivery if they are damaged, defective, or the wrong item was delivered.

Opened, used, or tampered perfume bottles cannot be returned due to hygiene and safety reasons.

3. Refund Process

Once the returned product passes inspection, refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method.

4. Exchange Policy

Exchanges are available only for damaged or incorrect products, subject to stock availability.

5. Return Shipping

If the return is due to our error, return shipping charges will be borne by WITH YOU.
